# Confirmed review fixes in 0.7.1

The 0.7.1 candidate addresses the confirmed defects identified during independent testing of commit d5c4b73.

The fixes cover report localization and numbering, matrix clipping/RTL behavior, explicit treatment of unplotted authority data, switch-point boundary behavior, incomplete budget visibility, escalation-pack identity and scope, criterion-polarity anchor acknowledgement, export-menu simplification, and complete locale-catalog parity testing.

The candidate deliberately preserves SULTAN's core separation between strategic value, capability/readiness, and authorization. Mandatory requirements remain outside discretionary ranking, and authority is descriptive rather than predictive.
