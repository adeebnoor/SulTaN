# Strategy design studio — 0.10.0-beta

SULTAN now connects opportunity reasoning to its existing choices, authority model, indicators, initiatives and funding records.

## Method and user journey

1. Diagnose the institutional problem, scope, current evidence, transferable lessons and risk appetite.
2. Compare target segments and buyers. Record demand evidence and institutional advantage. A national transformation agenda is a hypothesis to substantiate, not proof of an opportunity. Contract size alone is insufficient.
3. Compare build, partner and acquire. A partnership needs reciprocal value and evidence of feasibility. Acquisition requires diligence and an integration plan. These are alternatives, not defaults for every institution.
4. Assess the existing operating model. Reuse it if adequate; otherwise distinguish governance and decision rights from management, staffing, sourcing and interim arrangements.
5. Create linked initiative cards: sponsor, scope, benefit owner, measurement definition, activities, outputs, acceptance, unit-based costs, risk owners and warning thresholds. Existing KPI baselines, annual targets, dependencies and funding remain canonical.
6. Review the evidence gaps and hand over strategy design. Internal execution, ongoing advisory support and implementation engagements are separately identified.

This general method was synthesised from the owner's expert advice and privately supplied strategy-method examples. No uploaded document, confidential client data, source slide, original budget or original initiative content is included in the repository or product.

## Model and limits

`project.strategy` is an optional version-1 extension. Older projects receive a disabled empty extension. Enabling the studio is explicit. The importer validates every nested field, enum, date, range, duplicate identity and collection size. User-entered data is retained through JSON export/import and AI redrafting remaps stable relationships.

Commercial annual value = annual contract value × paying customer count. Recurring value = annual value × recurring share. Gross profit = annual value × gross margin. These are scenarios, never cash, bookings, forecasts validated by SULTAN, or additions to the funding pool. Public-value opportunities do not require revenue. Unknowns stay unknown; known zero remains zero. Evidence status is a user declaration, not verification.

Detailed costs = quantity × unit cost. They explain the existing annual initiative budget. An explicit confirmation copies estimates into the represented years; it neither changes other years nor approves funding. The checker flags incomplete estimates, horizon errors and budget mismatches. There is no automatic aggregation of overlapping opportunity revenue.

The studio uses existing opt-in AI transport. Review prompts receive selected opportunities and linked cards only. It does not transmit project data by itself. The source attachments are not uploaded to the AI service.

## Verification

Model tests cover migration, roundtrips, missing versus zero, public-value treatment, partnership and acquisition requirements, inactive opportunities, costs, date/range validation, escaping, AI remapping and review freshness. The CI browser journey exercises both languages, consecutive edits, reloads, route-dependent fields, existing-model reuse, budget transfer, reports and mobile layouts.

Field completeness is not approval. No product-market-fit, financial forecast, regulatory compliance, security certification or international competitiveness claim follows from these software checks. External sector review and measured customer pilots remain necessary.

## Goal-first generation

The studio starts with one goal per line (up to ten). One action creates a linked draft of proposed indicators, benchmark research plans, annual milestones, three-phase execution initiatives, dependencies, enablers, activity plans, cost worksheets and risk cards. Six bilingual measurement recipes cover growth, efficiency, service, skills, risk and general outcomes. The metric family is a transparent starting hypothesis, not a semantic understanding guarantee. Proposed roles and schedule dates require review.

With the existing AI consent enabled, new objectives can receive context-specific drafting through the existing AI transport. The model works on an isolated seed of new objectives; existing records and measured data are never replaced. A response that omits an objective is completed locally. A failed or truncated response falls back to a clearly disclosed local draft. Without consent there is no network request. Concurrent edits prevent an in-flight result from overwriting the current project.

Repeated generation completes missing links without duplicating goals or rewriting edited cards. Baselines, numeric targets, budgets and observed benchmark values are not invented. Local benchmark records are research plans, marked unchecked with no fabricated source URL; peer selection, verified comparison data and suitability review remain required. This is an editable strategy draft, not autonomous approval or execution.
